# Improvements of the newest strategies

Generated by `improvements.py`; the request is in `PROMPT.md`.

## Setup

- **Tickers:** the 2000 stocks and ETFs of round 3 (`results/run_20261019_top3_2000`), 2 bps per buy or sell, $10,000 per ticker. 109 trading days from 2026-05-13 to 2026-10-16; returns are totals, not annualized. The second half starts at the close of 2026-07-31.
- **Fits:** at every bar, the last 1560 bars of each price field (high, open, close), run on its own; tables use the mean of the three fields.
  - **kernel:** as in round 3.
  - **kernel, volume weights:** each bar's volume is its weight, divided by the window's total volume; each bar's gaussian kernel enters the density and the CDF with that weight. The bandwidth is unchanged.
  - **Bernstein:** the support is fixed at [lowest − 1/1560, highest + 1/1560] of the window (0.000641 on each side), with the degree chosen automatically.
- **Strategies:** all buy below the fitted 25% quantile.
  - **q25 / sell, no rule:** round 3 when the fit is the kernel and the sell is q90.
  - **1. tim_20d > 85.3%, entry only** and **2. tim_all < 50%, entry only:** buy only while the plain strategy (same fit and sell threshold) has held the ticker more than 85.3% of the last 1560 bars, or less than 50% of all bars since trading started.
  - **new: either gate:** buy while gate 1 OR gate 2 holds.
  - **3. cap + stop:** sell, and do not buy, while the strategy has held the ticker more than 50% of the last 1560 bars (after 1560 bars of trading); sell 6% below the buy price and stay out until the close is back above that level.
- **Sell thresholds:** the fitted 90%, 95% and 99% quantiles, and *max*: the highest value of the price field over the previous 1560 bars, not counting the current bar (with it, the close could never be above it).
- **Bernstein mode filters:** from the close's Bernstein fits.
  - **band:** at each day's close, the mode is refitted 20 times, leaving out one of the window's days each time. The mode is *falling* when it is below the 10% quantile of the previous day's 20 leave-one-day-out modes, and *rising* when it is above their 90% quantile.
  - **trajectory:** the mode is *falling* when it fell over each of the last two days (now < 1 day ago < 2 days ago, 78 bars a day), and *rising* when it rose over both.
  - **Two readings.** *no buy if falling, no sell if rising* is the usual guard for this strategy: its buys fire during drops and its sells during rallies. *no sell if falling, no buy if rising* is the request as written. Both block only buys and signal sells; the cap and the stop always apply.
  - **How often they are on** (share of bars, mean over tickers): band falling 11%, rising 11%; trajectory falling 25%, rising 25%.
- **Checks:** the plain kernel q90 strategy reproduces round 3 (largest per-ticker gap 0.0e+00), and kernel q90 cap + stop reproduces the stop-rules run (0.0e+00).

## Caveats

- **One period, one market:** about five months, with a falling second half that favours any rule holding more cash.
- **Many combinations:** with this many tried on the same tickers and period, the best ones are partly luck. The 85.3% gate was itself chosen on the first half.
- **Max sell:** selling only at a new 20-day high often holds for weeks; it behaves more like buy and hold with a dip entry.
