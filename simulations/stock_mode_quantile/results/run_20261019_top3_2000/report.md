## Setup

- **Combinations:** the three that ranked first, with 2 bps per trade, in the second round (`results/run_20261019_seed2027`). Each buys when the close falls below the fitted 25% quantile and sells when it rises above the fitted 90% quantile of the last 1560 bars (about 20 trading days), fitted with `modefinder.ModeFinder(..., smooth_option="kernel")`. They differ only in the price field: high, open, close.
- **Tickers:** 2000 picked at random (seed 2028) from 2533 eligible files out of 13383: 1501 stocks and 499 ETFs. Eligible means at least 5000 bars, a median close of at least $5 and a median dollar volume of at least $100,000 per bar.
- **Period:** trading starts once the window has filled (mostly 2026-05-13 21:55) and runs to the end of each file (mostly 2026-10-16 21:55).
- **Money and costs:** $10,000 per ticker, long only, all in or all out, 2 bps per buy or sell. The portfolio splits $10,000 equally across all 2000 tickers, and each part trades on its own.
- **Fit and fills:** refitted at every bar. The decision uses bar t's close and a fit on bars up to t, and fills at the open of bar t + 1.

## Caveats

- **Selection bias:** the three combinations were chosen as the best of 216 on 30 tickers over this same period. Most of these tickers are new, but the dates are the same, so this is an out-of-sample test across tickers, not across time.
- **Ranking is in sample:** the top targets are ranked after the fact. A ticker that did well here is not shown to do well next period.
- **Costs and fills:** fills at the next bar's open with a flat cost per trade. Spreads, slippage and market impact on less liquid names are not modelled beyond that.
- **Prices:** dividends are ignored, prices may not be adjusted for splits (see the ⚠ flags), and the data may have survivorship bias.
