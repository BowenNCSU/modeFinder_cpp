# q05/q95 with a time-in-market cap and a stop loss

Generated by `stop_rules.py`; the request is in `PROMPT.md`.

## Setup

- **Tickers:** the 2000 stocks and ETFs of round 3 (`simulations/stock_mode_quantile/results/run_20261019_top3_2000`), 2 bps per buy or sell, $10,000 per ticker. 109 trading days from 2026-05-13 to 2026-10-16; returns are totals over this period, not annualized.
- **New strategy** (`q05/q95 + tim + stop`): at every bar the last 1560 bars (20 trading days) of the price field are fitted with the kernel method.
  - **Buy:** all in when the close is below the fitted 5% quantile.
  - **Sell:** all out when the close is above the fitted 95% quantile.
  - **Time in market:** once the strategy has traded for 1560 bars (20 days), it sells, and does not buy, as long as it held the ticker more than 50% of the last 1560 bars.
  - **Stop loss:** it sells when the close is more than 6% below its buy price, and does not buy again as long as the close stays below that stop level.
  - Orders are decided at a bar's close and filled at the next open. Each price field (high, open, close) is fitted and run on its own; the tables use the mean of the three.
- **Comparisons:** each rule on its own, a stop that allows buying again at once, q25/q90 with both rules, round 3's q25/q90, and buy and hold. The rerun of round 3 reproduces its returns (largest gap 0.0e+00).
- **Second half:** from the close of 2026-07-31, the split used in the threshold study.

## Caveats

- **One period:** about five months, and the market fell in the second half, which favours any rule that holds more cash.
- **Rules chosen after the fact:** the 6% stop and the 50% cap were set after seeing round 3 and the threshold study on the same tickers and period, so this is not an out-of-sample test.
- **Fills:** a stop sells at the next open, which can be well below the stop level after a gap.
