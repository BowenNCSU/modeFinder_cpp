## Setup

- **Strategy:** the three combinations of `simulations/stock_mode_quantile/results/run_20261019_top3_2000` (q25/q90 · high, open, close · 1560 bars · kernel) on the same 2000 tickers, 2 bps per trade. The rerun reproduces that run's returns (largest gap 0.0e+00).
- **Features**, each computed at every bar from the bars up to it:
  - `skew_d20`, `skew_dall`: skewness of daily close-to-close log returns over the last 20 days, or over all days so far.
  - `skew_h20`, `skew_hall`: skewness of hourly log returns within the session (overnight gaps left out) over the last 140 hours, or all so far.
  - `tim_all`, `tim_20d`: the share of bars the strategy has held the ticker since trading started, or over the last 1560 bars.
  - Others: `mom_5d`, `mom_20d` (price change over 390 / 1560 bars), `vol_20d` (annualized sd of the last 20 daily returns), `band_width` ((fitted q90 − fitted q25) / close) and `strat_ret` (the strategy's return on the ticker so far).
  - Strategy features (`tim_*`, `band_width`, `strat_ret`) are averaged over the three price fields in the panel; the gated strategy uses each field's own values. Values are fractions: `tim_all < 0.5` means held less than 50% of the time.
- **Period split:** 109 trading days from 2026-05-13 to 2026-10-16, split at 2026-07-31.
  - **Train:** checkpoints whose 20-day outcome ends by the split (67,761 ticker-days). Thresholds are chosen here.
  - **Test:** checkpoints from the split on (69,873 ticker-days).
  - **Mid:** each ticker at the split day, with the outcome from there to the end of the data.
- **Outcome:** the strategy's return over the next 20 trading days, starting at the next open (mean of the three fields). "Profitable" means above 0; a ticker the strategy stays out of returns exactly 0.
- **Search:** 3,272 rules: single thresholds at the train deciles of each feature, pairs of thresholds at the train 30/50/70% points, and the natural cut points (0 for skew, momentum and strategy return, 50% for time in market). Searched rules must pass at least 20% of train rows and are ranked by the train mean return of the rows they pass.

## Caveats

- **Many rules, one period:** the best train rules are the best of thousands, so their train numbers are optimistic. Only the test, mid and second-half columns are out of sample, and only in time: the tickers and the three combinations are the same.
- **Overlapping outcomes:** 20-day outcomes on consecutive days overlap, so the panel holds far fewer independent observations than rows.
- **One market regime:** about five months of data. A threshold that works here may only reflect what this period rewarded.
